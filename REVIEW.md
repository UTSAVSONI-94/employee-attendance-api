### Looked at and decided were fine

- `load_dotenv()` (default `override=False`) already lets real environment variables win over `.env`; kept (made `override=False` explicit).
- `IST = timezone(timedelta(hours=5, minutes=30))` is correct; reused.
- `MONGO_URI` / `MONGO_DB` read from the environment with local defaults; not hard-coded credentials.
- `list_attendance` building `date` with `$gte` / `$lte` on strings: correct, because `YYYY-MM-DD` strings sort chronologically. Kept, but the dates are now validated first.
- `compute_work_hours`' formula itself (`seconds / 3600`) is right; only the rounding and second-truncation were wrong.
- Synchronous PyMongo inside `def` endpoints is fine: FastAPI runs them in its threadpool, so concurrent requests do not block each other; correctness under concurrency comes from unique indexes and conditional updates, not from the driver.
